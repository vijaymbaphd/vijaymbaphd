## Vijayakumar P

Assistant Professor. I teach Performance Management and Advanced Business Analytics to MBA and EMBA cohorts, and I publish the teaching material openly instead of keeping it behind a course login.

Everything here is meant to be opened, forked and taught from.

### What I publish

**Open-access books.** Full-length Quarto books on business analytics, data science, HR analytics and management. Source and rendered site are both public, so a chapter can be lifted into someone else's course without asking.

**Analytics Lab.** Single-file interactive HTML tools built for the classroom: sampling distributions, regression diagnostics, factor structure. No install and no server. Open the file in a browser and it runs.

**Research code.** R and Quarto pipelines from my own work on the Performance Pressure scale, covering item generation through EFA, CFA and measurement invariance, with the reporting scripted end to end.

**Journal Finder.** A small tool for matching a manuscript to plausible outlets.

### Background

UGC JRF. NET three times, SET three times. Microsoft Certified: Power BI Data Analyst Associate, plus certifications from IIM Kozhikode, IIT Kanpur and IIT Roorkee. My research sits in performance pressure and organisational behaviour, mostly scale development and SEM.

Working tools: R, Python, Power BI, STATA, SPSS, Tableau, Looker Studio.

Teaching view: the best way to learn analytics is to do it on real questions.
